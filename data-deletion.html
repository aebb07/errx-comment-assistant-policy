User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 21, 2026

ERRX Comment Assistant is a locally operated tool used to manage comments posted on @errx.vu. You can request removal of records about your comments stored by this tool.

How to request deletion

  1. Email aebbp07@gmail.com with the subject ERRX Comment Assistant — Data Deletion Request.
  2. Provide your Instagram username and, if possible, the link to the relevant post or comment.
  3. The Operator may request reasonable information to locate or verify your request. Never share your password, security code or access token.
  4. The Operator will review your request and remove eligible local records, subject to applicable law, and respond as appropriate.

What can be deleted?

Matching saved comment text, usernames, comment metadata, draft replies and review status in the tool's local database, subject to applicable obligations or exceptions. This prototype does not yet provide automatic deletion through this webpage; the Operator processes requests manually.

What remains elsewhere?

Removing local records does not delete the original comment from Instagram or undo a reply already posted there. Provider-side records, where applicable, are subject to those providers' policies and rights processes.

Stop access to your account

Account owners who have authorized the tool can review or remove app access using Instagram's Apps and websites settings. Revoking app access does not itself erase copies already stored locally.
